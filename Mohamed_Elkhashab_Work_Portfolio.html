Workflow design / 01

Reusable audit workflows

Problem
Recurring document reviews needed consistent evidence rules and follow-up.
What I designed
A reusable audit framework with source checks, scoring rules, missing-information handling, and corrective-action documentation.
How AI was used
Structured prompts supported document organization, evidence extraction, and draft findings.
Human validation / oversight
I defined the criteria, reviewed outputs, and kept missing evidence separate from confirmed deficiencies.
Operational value
Created a repeatable review method and a clear path from source evidence to reviewable findings.

Workflow design / 02

Action-plan continuity monitoring

Problem
Meeting decisions could be lost between action plans and later monitoring.
What I designed
A three-question review tool tracing agreed actions into formal plans and subsequent progress reviews.
How AI was used
AI helped translate the handoff problem into conditional checks and evidence fields.
Human validation / oversight
I set applicability rules, required matching review periods, and distinguished missing records from confirmed noncompletion.
Operational value
Made potential handoff gaps and unresolved evidence needs visible for follow-up. The template was produced; recurring use and closure gains were not established.

Workflow design / 03

Operating playbook and coverage planning

Problem
Unclear ownership and vacancies complicated handoffs and continuity.
What I designed
An operating playbook, responsibility matrices, backup assignments, and vacancy scenarios for leadership review.
How AI was used
AI helped structure requirements into workflow cards, checklists, ownership comparisons, and coverage options.
Human validation / oversight
I defined role boundaries, checked qualification and access requirements, and kept workload assumptions separate from measured capacity.
Operational value
Produced reviewable operating guidance and exposed coverage constraints before implementation. Adoption and staffing-efficiency gains were not measured.

Workflow design / 04

Monitoring redesign and training resources

Problem
Monthly monitoring and deeper quarterly analysis needed clearer expectations.
What I designed
A training deck explaining review cadence, progress monitoring, decisions, and action-plan follow-through.
How AI was used
Generative AI supported organizing process rules into explanations and a training structure.
Human validation / oversight
I refined the wording and preserved ongoing monthly monitoring while distinguishing quarterly analysis and decisions.
Operational value
Created a structured resource for consistent guidance. Training delivery, adoption, and learner outcomes were not established by the completed deck.

Workflow design / 05

Human validation and AI exception handling

Problem
AI drafts could misread missing data, discard partial evidence, or change unrelated content.
What I designed
Source-verification rules, field-level exceptions, partial-evidence preservation, and targeted correction instructions.
How AI was used
Iterative prompt refinement constrained how drafts handled incomplete evidence and revisions.
Human validation / oversight
I identified unsupported statements, checked sources, authorized exceptions, and limited corrections to affected content.
Operational value
Preserved supported findings and corrected unsupported interpretations. No overall AI accuracy rate is claimed.
